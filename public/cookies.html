Cookie Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

There's no cookie-consent banner on this site. Not because it was skipped — because every storage mechanism here is strictly necessary for the product to work, and none of it is tracking, advertising, or analytics. That's the actual reason, stated plainly rather than assumed.

1. The one cookie

NamePurposeDurationSet when
fm_sessKeeps you signed in — an opaque session reference, not your email or password12 hours, or 30 days if "keep me signed in" is checkedSigning in with an emailed code, a saved password, or Google (server-backed mode only — see Privacy Policy §2)

This cookie is HttpOnly (invisible to page JavaScript), Secure (HTTPS only), and SameSite=Strict (never sent to any other site). It is strictly necessary — the sign-in feature cannot work without it — which is exactly the category of cookie that GDPR's ePrivacy rules and similar laws exempt from requiring a consent banner.

2. Local storage (not a cookie, but the same idea)

Your browser's localStorage holds a few keys, all functional, none of them sent to any server:

KeyPurpose
finmodels.accountsDevice-local account records (§2.1 of the Privacy Policy) — never leaves your browser
finmodels.sessionRemembers that you're signed in, on this device
finmodels.countryYour chosen market, so rate/currency defaults don't reset every visit
finmodels.historyYour saved analyses, kept locally so they survive a page refresh

3. What is not here

No Google Analytics, no advertising pixels, no third-party tracking cookies, no cross-site tracking of any kind. If that changes in the future, this page — and this section specifically — will be updated first, and a consent mechanism will be added before any non-essential cookie is set, not after.

4. Third-party scripts that don't set cookies on you

The terminal loads its Python runtime and charting library from jsDelivr and cdn.plot.ly. These are code libraries your browser executes locally — they don't set tracking cookies for this site, and no personal data is sent to them as part of loading them.

5. Your control

Because the only cookie here is functionally required for sign-in, the way to refuse it is simply not to sign in — the free models and guest mode work without it. You can clear fm_sess and every localStorage key at any time through your browser's own site-data settings.
