2. The two account modes, and what each collects

The Service has two distinct account modes. Which one applies depends on whether the deployment has server-side auth configured; the sign-in screen tells you which mode is active.

2.1 Device-local mode

Your account (email, name, and a password hash) is created and stored entirely in your browser's localStorage. The password is never transmitted anywhere — it is hashed on your device (PBKDF2-SHA256, 150,000 iterations, a random salt per account) and the hash never leaves your browser either. I have no server-side copy of a device-local account and cannot see, recover, or delete it remotely — clearing your browser's site data removes it completely.

2.2 Server-backed mode (email code / Google / password)

If you sign in with an emailed one-time code, a saved password, or Google, the following is stored server-side, in a Redis database (Upstash), until you ask me to delete it:

FieldSourceNotes
Email addressYou, or your Google accountUsed as your account identifier

PasswordYouStored only as a salted scrypt hash — never in plain text, never visible to me

Signing in issues a random 32-byte session token, set as an fm_sess cookie (HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Strict) — it does not contain your email or any other readable data, only an opaque reference the server looks up. It is never readable by page scripts and is sent only to this site, never to any third party.

3. The company-filing analyzer (IB desk)

The PDF file you upload never leaves your browser. It is parsed entirely client-side, in a Python runtime compiled to WebAssembly running inside the page you're already on.

If you use one of the two paid models (Ind AS hidden-debt normalizer, reverse-DCF), the extracted numeric figures your browser pulled from the filing (e.g. revenue, debt, lease figures) — not the file itself — are sent to the server so that specific calculation can run, and are not retained afterward. Every free model runs entirely in your browser with nothing sent anywhere.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. I do not receive, see, or store your card, UPI, or bank details — Razorpay handles that directly and passes back only a payment confirmation, which I use to activate your plan. Razorpay's own privacy policy governs the payment step itself.

5. What I do not collect

  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind currently run on this site.
  • No behavioral profiling, no ad targeting, no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No plaintext passwords, ever, in any mode.

Your IP address is used only transiently, to enforce rate limits on abuse (e.g. "30 requests/minute per IP") — it is held for the length of that rate-limit window (seconds to a day) and is not logged permanently or linked to your account.

This applies regardless of where you are — including if GDPR (EU) or CCPA (California) would otherwise apply to you.

8. Data retention

  • Sessions: expire after 12 hours, or 30 days if you check "keep me signed in."
