# SEO strategy

Research-backed plan for ranking FINMODELS TERMINAL. Written 2026-09-14 from
four parallel SERP/keyword/backlink research passes. Everything here is either
**already built** (linked to the code), **needs a decision from you**, or is
**off-page work no script can do**.

---

## 1. The honest verdict on "financial modeling"

**Do not target it.** Page one is Wikipedia, Wall Street Prep, Corporate
Finance Institute, the CFA Institute, Mergers & Inquisitions and IBM — every
one a DR 80+ domain with a decade of links. Two independent reasons to walk
away:

- **Authority.** A site with a handful of pages and no backlink profile is not
  displacing Wikipedia on a head term in any realistic timeframe.
- **Intent.** That SERP is 100% learn/certify. Nobody typing "financial
  modeling" wants a WebAssembly Black-Scholes runner. Even a miracle ranking
  would convert at roughly zero.

Treat the phrase as branding copy, not a keyword target. The traffic that
actually converts here is **tool intent** — people who want to *compute*
something, not read a definition.

## 2. What was built for this

Ranking for tool-intent queries needs one indexable URL per query, carrying
real content. The terminal is a single WebAssembly app with nowhere to put
that, which is why these exist:

| Page | Target queries | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| [`/reverse-dcf-calculator`](../public/reverse-dcf-calculator.html) | reverse dcf calculator, market implied growth rate | Weakest high-value SERP found. The #1 result is an email-gated Excel file; almost no live free tool exists. |
| [`/ifrs-16-hidden-debt-calculator`](../public/ifrs-16-hidden-debt-calculator.html) | operating lease capitalization, ifrs 16 hidden debt | Genuinely unoccupied. Every ranking "IFRS 16 calculator" is *accounting-compliance* software; the analyst/valuation angle exists only as prose (Damodaran, Footnotes Analyst). |
| [`/monte-carlo-stock-price-simulator`](../public/monte-carlo-stock-price-simulator.html) | monte carlo stock price simulation, gbm simulator | That SERP currently returns **no calculator at all** — Medium posts and IEEE papers. |
| [`/black-scholes-calculator`](../public/black-scholes-calculator.html) | black scholes calculator, greeks calculator | Highest volume of the four, hardest SERP (Omni, Zerodha, Macroption). Long payback — included because real SciPy Greeks beat the thin JS incumbents on quality. |

All four are generated by
[`scripts/build_landing_pages.py`](../scripts/build_landing_pages.py) and
deep-link into the live model via `/?m=<MNEMONIC>`.

**Adding a fifth page:** add a dict to `PAGES`, run the generator, commit. The
sitemap updates itself and `tests/test_landing_pages.py` guards the result.

## 3. Next pages to build, in priority order

Ordered by (gap × demand) ÷ effort, from the SERP research:

1. **`/value-at-risk-calculator`** — incumbents are parametric-only with three
   inputs. Shipping historical + Monte Carlo + CVaR is a real quality delta.
2. **`/wacc-calculator`** and **`/capm-calculator`** — ship as a cross-linked
   pair. The SERPs are saturated but the incumbents are ~200-word pages, so
   they're winnable slowly on quality. Low expected value per page; cheap.
3. **`/efficient-frontier-calculator`** — *skip for now.* Portfolio Visualizer
   and testfol.io are real products with data moats.
4. **`/heston-model-calculator`** — near-zero competition (the only web
   calculator is an old Java page) but also near-zero volume. Build it for
   credibility signalling, not traffic. Do it last.
5. **A generic `/dcf-calculator`** — *skip* until there's a live financials
   feed. Every winner on that SERP (Alphaspread, GuruFocus, dcfcalc) autoloads
   company financials from a ticker; a blank-input DCF cannot compete.

### The Ind AS 116 finding worth acting on

There is real search demand for "Ind AS 116 calculator" — but those searchers
are **accountants doing compliance**, wanting an ROU asset schedule,
amortization table and journal entries. The hidden-debt normalizer is an
*analyst* tool in the Damodaran tradition, and the analyst-side query has
almost no traffic behind it (Damodaran's own PDFs rank there, which tells you
the volume is small).

**Recommendation:** add a compliance-shaped ROU/amortization schedule output
alongside the existing normalizer. That converts a zero-traffic moat into
"ind as 116 lease calculator" and "rou asset lease liability calculator",
which have genuine demand and beatable competitors.

## 4. Decisions needed from you

These are blocked on you, not on code.

- **Custom domain — the biggest single lever.** The site is on
  `financial-models-six.vercel.app`. No incumbent on any SERP researched sits
  on a free PaaS subdomain. A real domain should be treated as a precondition
  for ranking, not an optimization. It costs money and changes every canonical
  URL, so it's your call — but it's the highest-leverage item on this page.
  When you do it: every `SITE` constant, canonical, sitemap entry and og:url
  needs updating, plus 301s from the old host.
- **Google Search Console.** I could not find a verification token in the repo
  and cannot verify from here whether it's set up (DNS and Google-Analytics
  verification leave no trace in the code). If it isn't done, do it — without
  it you're optimizing blind. Bing Webmaster Tools is worth the extra ten
  minutes.

## 5. Backlinks — off-page, sequenced

No script can do this part. Ordered by (effort × realistic payoff). Nothing
here is paid or risks a penalty.

**Days 1–15 — foundation, needs no audience**
- **MCP registries.** `awesome-mcp-servers` has a live Finance & Fintech
  section; also the official `modelcontextprotocol/servers` community list,
  plus glama.ai, smithery.ai and pulsemcp.com. This is the easiest cluster of
  real links available — almost nobody has a quant-models MCP server, and
  this project already ships one.
- **Pyodide `related-projects.md`** — a PR against Pyodide's own docs. No
  formal process; high authority; genuinely on-topic.
- **`awesome-wasm`**, AlternativeTo (list as a Bloomberg/Koyfin alternative),
  SaaSHub.
- Tighten the GitHub README first — every awesome-list reviewer judges it.

**Days 16–40 — lead with the engineering story, not finance**
Real CPython running Heston and Monte Carlo in a browser is interesting
independent of finance, and these are far easier first links than finance
sites: WebAssembly Weekly, PyCoder's Weekly, Python Weekly, Console.dev,
r/webassembly and r/Python's showcase thread.

**Days 41–65 — the one asset worth building**
A public **Ind AS 116 / IFRS 16 hidden-debt study across the Nifty 500**:
compute lease-adjusted debt and restated leverage for every company, publish
the full table free with methodology. It's original data nobody else has,
directly quotable, and exactly what Indian finance writers cite. This is the
single best link magnet available to this project.

Supporting: a public **Hull benchmark suite** (every model's output against
Hull's textbook worked examples — a citable correctness claim almost no free
calculator makes), and a quarterly **reverse-DCF table of market-implied
growth rates** for the Nifty 50 and S&P 500.

**Days 66–80 — launch, once there are proof points to link**
Show HN (link the tool, blank text field, backstory as the first comment),
then Product Hunt.

**Days 81–90 — slow burn**
`awesome-quant` (submit last; it's the pickiest list and wants repo activity —
its CONTRIBUTING.md does allow commercial tools with a real free tier).
Ten individually-written emails to university librarians who maintain finance
LibGuides — several already link free valuation calculators like MoneyChimp.
Email the named librarian on the guide, not a generic address; expect roughly
one in eight to land. Begin answering Quantitative Finance Stack Exchange
questions, disclosing affiliation.

**Communities that will ban you for self-promotion:** r/algotrading and
r/quant both remove vendor/tool posts. Treat them as answer-only surfaces.
Check each sidebar yourself before posting.

**Not worth it:** G2 and Capterra (review-gated, nofollow, and you won't
accumulate review volume at $29/mo self-serve), BetaList's paid fast track,
Slant (largely dormant).

## 6. Guardrails

[`scripts/test_seo.js`](../scripts/test_seo.js) enforces the invariants that
rot silently — canonical self-reference, one `<h1>` per page, title/description
length, no accidental `noindex`, JSON-LD validity and `@id` resolution, no
broken internal links, sitemap coverage, and that every `?m=` deep link points
at a mnemonic the terminal actually knows.

Run it with the rest:

```bash
node scripts/test_seo.js
```

Two rules worth keeping:

- **Never fabricate structured data.** `aggregateRating` stays out until there
  is a real review corpus — invented rich-result markup is the fastest way to
  earn a manual action. The `offers` blocks carry the real published price and
  **must be updated whenever pricing changes** (`tests/test_landing_pages.py`
  pins them to the actual plan).
- **Never advertise a gated model as free.** Reverse DCF and the hidden-debt
  normalizer need Analyst Pro; their landing pages say so above the fold, and
  a test enforces it.
